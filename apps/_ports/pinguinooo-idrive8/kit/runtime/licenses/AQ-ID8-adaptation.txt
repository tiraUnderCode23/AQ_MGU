AQ adaptations include bounded renderer area, type-based cell mapping,
no native Graphic fallback on ID8, explicit typed read failures and canvas
gating. Shift Lights adds hello-negotiated ID8 map/HUD rectangles, virtual
artwork scaling, cache removal before reconnect, and bounded read-only
RPM/oil freshness checks using only its already-declared getters. ID8
requires opening the app after each head-unit start; its startup preference
is informational and cannot be enabled. ID7 startup behavior is retained.

The unmodified Shift Lights cluster_render_client.js and its application
NOTICE retain the original MPL-2.0 attribution to Robert Nisipeanu. The
complete license is installed as Cluster-Render-Client-MPL-2.0.txt. Original
client commit: 793da1ec9c8f761fe9aa319cddb48544bada40b6, in
https://github.com/robertnisipeanu/idrive-apps . The ID8 target contract was
reviewed in the same repository at 59140a978e3d5e2c8c183bdd2fbb9a4682be466a.

SDK-free contract tests do not establish live vehicle compatibility.
